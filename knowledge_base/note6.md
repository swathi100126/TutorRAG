
# Note 06: Respiration

Title: Respiration in Living Organisms
Topic Tag: respiration
Section Number: 1.6

## Definition
Respiration is the process of breaking down food to release energy for cellular activities.

## Types of Respiration
1. Aerobic respiration: Takes place in the presence of oxygen.
2. Anaerobic respiration: Takes place without oxygen.

## Chemical Equation
Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy

## Key Points
- Aerobic respiration releases more energy than anaerobic respiration.
- Glycolysis takes place in the cytoplasm.
- Aerobic respiration mainly occurs in mitochondria.

## Common Mistake
Breathing and respiration are not the same. Breathing involves air movement, while respiration releases energy from food.
