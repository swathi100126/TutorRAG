
# Note 09: Transportation in Plants

Title: Transportation in Plants
Topic Tag: plant_transportation
Section Number: 1.9

## Definition
Transportation in plants is the movement of water, minerals and food to different parts of the plant.

## Main Tissues
- Xylem: Transports water and minerals from roots to other plant parts.
- Phloem: Transports food from leaves to other plant parts.

## Important Processes
- Transpiration: Loss of water vapour from aerial parts of plants, mainly through stomata.
- Translocation: Movement of food through phloem.

## Example
Water absorbed by roots travels to leaves through xylem.

## Common Mistake
Xylem transports water and minerals, whereas phloem transports food.
