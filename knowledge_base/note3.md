
# Note 03: Autotrophic Nutrition

Title: Autotrophic Nutrition
Topic Tag: autotrophic_nutrition
Section Number: 1.3

## Definition
Autotrophic nutrition is a mode of nutrition in which organisms prepare their own food using simple inorganic substances.

## Key Points
- Green plants are autotrophs.
- Photosynthesis is the main process of autotrophic nutrition.
- Chlorophyll absorbs sunlight.
- Carbon dioxide and water are used to prepare food.

## Example
Green plants prepare glucose through photosynthesis.

## Common Mistake
Plants do not obtain ready-made food from soil. They prepare their own food through photosynthesis.
