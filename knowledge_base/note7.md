
# Note 07: Human Respiratory System

Title: Human Respiratory System
Topic Tag: human_respiration
Section Number: 1.7

## Definition
The human respiratory system helps the body take in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide.

## Main Organs
- Nose: Filters incoming air.
- Trachea: Carries air towards the lungs.
- Bronchi: Carry air into each lung.
- Lungs: Main organs of breathing.
- Alveoli: Tiny air sacs where gas exchange occurs.

## Key Points
- Oxygen enters the blood through alveoli.
- Carbon dioxide moves from blood into alveoli.
- Alveoli have thin walls and a rich blood supply.

## Common Mistake
Gas exchange takes place mainly in alveoli, not in the trachea.
