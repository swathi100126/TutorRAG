
# Note 04: Photosynthesis

Title: Photosynthesis
Topic Tag: photosynthesis
Section Number: 1.4

## Definition
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare food using carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll.

## Requirements
- Sunlight
- Chlorophyll
- Carbon dioxide
- Water

## Chemical Equation
6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

## Key Points
- Photosynthesis mainly occurs in leaves.
- Chlorophyll absorbs light energy.
- Oxygen is released during the process.

## Common Mistake
Photosynthesis and respiration are different processes. Photosynthesis stores energy in food, while respiration releases energy from food.
