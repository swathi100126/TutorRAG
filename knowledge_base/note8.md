
# Note 08: Transportation in Humans

Title: Transportation in Human Beings
Topic Tag: human_transportation
Section Number: 1.8

## Definition
Transportation is the process of moving oxygen, nutrients and waste materials throughout the human body.

## Main Components
- Heart: Pumps blood.
- Blood: Transports oxygen, nutrients and wastes.
- Blood vessels: Carry blood throughout the body.

## Components of Blood
- RBCs: Carry oxygen.
- WBCs: Protect the body from infections.
- Platelets: Help in blood clotting.
- Plasma: Transports dissolved substances.

## Key Point
The human heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

## Common Mistake
Arteries carry blood away from the heart, while veins carry blood towards the heart.
