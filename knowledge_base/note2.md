
# Note 02: Nutrition

Title: Nutrition in Living Organisms
Topic Tag: nutrition
Section Number: 1.2

## Definition
Nutrition is the process by which organisms obtain food and use it for energy, growth and repair.

## Types of Nutrition
1. Autotrophic Nutrition: Organisms prepare their own food.
2. Heterotrophic Nutrition: Organisms depend on other organisms for food.

## Examples
- Green plants: Autotrophic nutrition.
- Humans: Heterotrophic nutrition.

## Common Mistake
Nutrition and digestion are not the same. Nutrition includes obtaining and using food, while digestion breaks complex food into simpler substances.
