
# Note 05: Human Digestive System

Title: Human Digestive System
Topic Tag: human_digestion
Section Number: 1.5

## Definition
Digestion is the process of breaking down complex food into simpler, soluble substances that the body can absorb.

## Main Organs
- Mouth: Chews food and mixes it with saliva.
- Oesophagus: Carries food to the stomach.
- Stomach: Helps digest proteins.
- Small intestine: Completes digestion and absorbs nutrients.
- Large intestine: Absorbs water.

## Example
Salivary amylase in the mouth begins the digestion of starch.

## Common Mistake
Most nutrient absorption occurs in the small intestine, not the stomach.
