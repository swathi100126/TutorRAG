
# Note 10: Excretion in Humans

Title: Human Excretory System
Topic Tag: human_excretion
Section Number: 1.10

## Definition
Excretion is the process of removing metabolic waste products from the body.

## Main Organs
- Kidneys: Filter blood and produce urine.
- Ureters: Carry urine to the urinary bladder.
- Urinary bladder: Stores urine.
- Urethra: Carries urine outside the body.

## Important Concept
A nephron is the structural and functional unit of the kidney.

## Steps of Urine Formation
1. Filtration.
2. Selective reabsorption.
3. Tubular secretion.

## Common Mistake
Kidneys produce urine, while the urinary bladder stores it.
