
# Note 01: Introduction to Life Processes

Title: Introduction to Life Processes
Topic Tag: life_processes
Section Number: 1.1

## Definition
Life processes are essential activities performed by living organisms to maintain life.

## Key Points
- Nutrition: Obtaining food.
- Respiration: Releasing energy from food.
- Transportation: Moving substances within the body.
- Excretion: Removing metabolic wastes.

## Example
Humans eat food, release energy through respiration and remove wastes through excretion.

## Common Mistake
Breathing and respiration are different. Breathing involves air movement, while respiration releases energy from food.
